8-Page Mini-Book Folding & Cutting Guide

Each sheet prints 8 pages on a single piece of standard US Letter paper (landscape).

  1. Fold Lengthwise ("Hot Dog"): Fold the sheet in half the long way (bringing top edge to bottom edge). Crease sharply and unfold.
  2. Fold Widthwise into Quarters: Fold the sheet in half the short way ("hamburger"), then fold each side in half again so the sheet has 4 equal columns. Crease well and unfold flat.
  3. Cut the Center Slit: Fold the paper in half "hamburger" style. Starting from the folded center spine, cut along the middle crease line across the two inner columns only (stop at the outer fold lines). Do not cut all the way through!
  4. Pop Open: Unfold the sheet flat, then fold it lengthwise ("hot dog style") again.
  5. Form the Cross: Push the outer left and right ends toward the center. The center slit will pop open into a diamond / plus sign (+).
  6. Fold into Booklet: Push the pages together to flatten into 4 leaves. Fold the Front Cover (Page 1) to the front, and your 8-page book is ready to read!
