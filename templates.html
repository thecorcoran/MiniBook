✂️ 8-Page Mini-Book Folding & Cutting Guide

Prints 8 pages on a single sheet of standard US Letter paper in landscape orientation.

  1. Fold Lengthwise ("Hot Dog Fold"): Fold the sheet in half lengthwise (bringing the top edge to the bottom edge). Crease firmly and unfold.
  2. Fold Widthwise into Quarters: Fold the sheet in half widthwise ("hamburger style"), then fold each half in half again to form 4 equal columns. Crease well and unfold flat.
  3. Cut the Center Slit: Fold the paper in half widthwise ("hamburger style"). Starting from the folded center spine, cut along the middle crease line across the two inner columns only (between Pages 5/6 & 2/1 and Pages 4/5 & 3/2). Stop at the outer fold lines.
  4. Pop Open the Cross: Unfold flat, then fold lengthwise along the horizontal crease. Holding both outer ends, push inwards toward the center. The center slit pops open into a 4-leaf plus sign (+).
  5. Fold into Booklet: Collapse the leaves together and fold the Front Cover (Page 1) over to the front. Your 8-page mini-book is assembled and ready to read!
